## Caveat (checked, not a live risk)

`roboflow_lochan`'s instance/image counts above are inflated by Roboflow's
own 3x augmentation (vertical flip + 30° rotation) applied to its train
split before export — the true count of *distinct* photographs behind
these numbers is closer to the raw project's 1,417 images, not 3,392.
This was initially flagged as a near-duplicate-leakage risk across our own
scene-based split, but verified otherwise: Roboflow's exported filenames
(`<id>_jpg.rf.<hash>.jpg`) share a stable `<id>` per original photo across
all its augmented copies, `scene_id_for()`'s default regex already keys on
exactly that `<id>`, and the real merge confirms 1,417 unique scenes
(matching the raw image count) with every augmented copy landing in the
same split as its original — see `results/split_manifest.csv`. What's
still worth remembering: these are Roboflow's own augmentation choices
(including the vertical flip this project's own policy avoids), not ours,
so don't treat the inflated instance counts as if they came from natural,
independent photographs when writing up per-class AP.
