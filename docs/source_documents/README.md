## Two things the guidelines deck settles, and one it does not

**Settled.** The title-page template is dated **1st October 2026**, which is
what `front_matter.md` now carries. The Investigation/Research structure in
Slide 4 matches this thesis chapter for chapter, including "Appendices – Ethics
Forms" as a named section. Submission is three elements: Report with ethics
forms, Code + Data (code compulsory, data optional), and a Poster in person or
a Recorded Presentation plus viva.

**Length — settled: there is no word limit (21 September 2026) and no page
limit (26 September 2026)**, both confirmed by the researcher. The deck agrees
by omission: Slide 24 says "Appendices do not count towards the page limit", but
no figure in words or pages appears anywhere in it. The assembled manuscript is about 21,500 words excluding appendices; the
per-chapter breakdown is in `docs/thesis/README.md`.

The signed ethics form, the University's guidelines deck and the original participant-sheet files are not included in this public copy.
