### The verification harness, and the two defects it caught

`tools/verify_decision_layer.sh` compiles the three *pure* decision-layer
sources for macOS and checks them against planted values — 45 checks, all
passing, output committed at
`results/objective3/decision_layer_verification.md`.

It exists because the offline pipeline had already lost a day to a
coordinate-space bug that did not crash: one pixel box applied to two
differently-sized arrays returned no reading for any frame, and that absence
was logged as a plausible finding about the LiDAR range ceiling. The live
pipeline carries the same hazard twice — letterbox padding, and a rotation
between the detector's frame and the depth map's — so both are pinned to
planted values.

It earned its keep immediately:

**1. The urgency function could not reach its own top warning band.** The first
form gave proximity and time-to-contact co-weights summing to one, capping a
stationary user's score below the "stop" threshold. A staircase half a metre
directly ahead scored as "close" purely because the user had stopped walking
towards it. Proximity now saturates the score on its own and closing speed
boosts it. **This is a demonstration-proof bug** — anyone demonstrating the
prototype is walking, so the broken case never occurs.

**2. `DepthSampler` clamped a fully off-frame ROI into a single corner pixel**
and returned that pixel's depth as though it were a reading of the object. It
now clips in fraction space and returns `nil` when nothing is left.

## 4. Things not to undo

Recorded here because each looks like a simplification and is not.

- **`FrameGeometry.landscapeROI(forPortrait:)` is a rotation, not an axis
  swap.** Dropping the flip mirrors every sampled region and returns a
  plausible wrong number. Pinned corner-by-corner in the harness.
- **Frames must be rotated upright before detection.** Measured offline: 0 of 4
  object-aimed frames detected as stored, 4 of 4 once rotated.
- **Urgency is `min(1, proximity + 0.35×ttc) × (0.6 + 0.4×hazard)`.** Not
  co-weights summing to 1 (unreachable top band), and hazard is not a plain
  multiplier (a chair 0.3 m from a shin is not a 0.3-urgency situation).
- **`AVSpeechSynthesizer` stays out of the per-frame path.** Hundreds of ms of
  first-utterance latency against a ~13 ms pipeline.
- **The audio session mixes rather than interrupts.** Interrupting silences a
  real user's VoiceOver.
- **Roll of −86° to −93° in portrait is correct** — ARKit defines the camera
  frame in landscape-right.
- **`results_long.csv` keeps the fallback-ROI rows.** Deleting them would
  remove the evidence for §4.2.1's coverage finding. `roi_source` is field 12.
